Origin

From a humble potato.

CRISPÉ began with a simple idea — to transform the humble potato into something worth remembering. Carefully sliced, golden-cooked and finished with bold flavours, every crisp is created around one obsession: the perfect crunch.

From the first slice to the final bite, CRISPÉ brings together craftsmanship, flavour and a love for beautifully simple things.

Craft — 01

Sliced with precision.

Every potato is cut to the same paper-thin measure, so each crisp fries evenly and holds the same honest crunch, bag after bag.

Craft — 02

Golden-cooked, slowly.

Low and slow in small batches, until every slice turns a deep, even gold — never rushed, never pale.
